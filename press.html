GeoGive Press Kit

FreeOpen web (PWA)Android app

One-liner

GeoGive connects neighbors to give away things they don't need to people nearby who do — free, local, and waste-free.

Boilerplate

GeoGive is a hyperlocal giving platform that makes it effortless to pass on unused items directly to neighbors. Founded on the belief that every unwanted item has a next home, GeoGive combines map-based discovery, built-in chat, and community trust scores to make giving as easy as throwing away — and far more rewarding. GeoGive is free to use, privacy-first, and available on the web and Android.

Founder quote
