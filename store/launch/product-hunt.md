# Product Hunt Launch Draft

## Name
GeoGive

## Tagline (60 chars)
Give away things you don't need to neighbors nearby

## Description
GeoGive is a hyperlocal giving platform: snap a photo of something you no longer need, drop a pin, and a neighbor who wants it messages you. No listings fees, no shipping, no landfill.

Built as an installable PWA + Android app with map-based discovery, real-time chat, trust scores from community ratings, offline support, and 5 languages.

## Maker comment
Hey PH! 👋 I kept seeing perfectly good stuff thrown out while people nearby were buying the same thing. Shipping kills most "free" marketplaces — so GeoGive is deliberately hyperlocal: everything is within walking distance.

It's a PWA first (installable, works offline), wrapped for Android. Everything free-to-give is free forever; optional paid boosts keep the lights on without ads or selling data.

Would love feedback on the giving flow — especially the request → chat → handoff loop!

## First comment topics
- Why hyperlocal beats shipping for free items
- Trust scores without surveillance
- Privacy-first: no trackers, no data selling
