# GeoGive — Google Play Store Listing

## App title (30 chars max)
GeoGive: Give & Get Free Stuff

## Short description (80 chars max)
Give away things you don't need to neighbors nearby. Free stuff, less waste.

## Full description
GeoGive connects people who have things to give with people who need them — right in your neighborhood.

🛋️ DECLUTTER WITH PURPOSE
That shelf you never use? A treasure for someone else. List items in seconds, chat with interested people, and hand things directly to neighbors who'll actually use them.

🗺️ HYPERLOCAL
See items on a map, filter by distance, and find giveaways within walking distance. No shipping, no hassle — just neighbors helping neighbors.

💬 SIMPLE & SAFE
Built-in chat keeps your phone number private. Rate givers, report problems, block users. Community guidelines keep GeoGive friendly for everyone.

✨ FEATURES
• Post unlimited free listings with photos
• Interactive map with distance search
• Real-time chat with item seekers
• Follow favorite givers and get a personal feed
• Trust scores from community ratings
• Available requests — ask for what you need
• Works offline, syncs when you're back
• 5 languages: English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Lietuvių
• Dark mode
• 100% free to give, 100% free to get

🌱 WHY IT MATTERS
Every year, millions of perfectly good items end up in landfills. GeoGive keeps useful things in use, saves you money, and builds real connections in your neighborhood.

Download GeoGive and give your stuff a second life today!
